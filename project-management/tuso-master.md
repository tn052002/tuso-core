# TUSO — Project Core

**Status:** Working foundation  
**Owner:** Product  
**Repository:** `tn052002/tuso-core`

## 1. Product in One Sentence

TUSO is a personal life-orientation system that helps people see their nature, understand their current season, and choose a more aligned next move.

> **See Your Nature. Know Your Season. Move With Life.**

## 2. Core Philosophy

TUSO offers **orientation, not prediction**.

It uses enduring pattern languages—initially Bát Tự, Tử Vi, and the I Ching—to help people recognize patterns in themselves and their circumstances. These systems are translated into grounded, contemporary language rather than presented as deterministic fortune-telling.

TUSO should help a person:

- see more clearly without surrendering agency;
- recognize conditions without treating them as fate;
- make a conscious move without being told what must happen;
- return to direct experience, reflection, and responsibility.

The product does not promise certainty. It creates a pause in which a person can orient.

## 3. The Three Questions

Every major product capability should serve one of three questions:

1. **Who am I? — Nature**
   - Enduring disposition, strengths, tensions, perception, decision style, relationships, work, and growth edges.
2. **Where am I? — Season**
   - The longer chapter and present conditions shaping what is timely, supported, constrained, emerging, or ending.
3. **How do I align now? — Move**
   - A contextual reflection and practical next move for a real question or moment.

These questions form the primary information architecture:

| Direction | Human question | Product expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nature | Who am I? | North / Personal Blueprint |
| Season | Where am I? | Current Chapter / Current Season |
| Move | How do I align now? | Oracle / Personal Meaning / Daily Move |

## 4. Product Promise

TUSO helps users leave with:

- language for what they are experiencing;
- a clearer relationship to their own nature;
- an understanding of the season they are moving through;
- one grounded next move;
- greater agency, not dependency on the product.

## 5. Target Users

### Primary audience

Adults roughly 28–45 who are:

- at a transition or decision point;
- outwardly functional but inwardly uncertain;
- tired of generic self-help or fatalistic readings;
- open to ancient wisdom when it is expressed in modern, psychologically useful language.

### Primary moments of need

- career or identity transition;
- relationship uncertainty;
- loss of direction or meaning;
- repeated patterns that the user cannot yet name;
- a decision that feels larger than rational comparison alone.

## 6. Product Principles

### 6.1 Orientation over prediction

Describe patterns, forces, tensions, and possibilities. Do not claim fixed outcomes or certainty about future events.

### 6.2 Agency over authority

The user remains the final interpreter and decision-maker. TUSO should never position itself as an unquestionable authority.

### 6.3 Recognition over labeling

A useful response should feel personally recognizable and reveal a dynamic—not merely assign a type.

### 6.4 Context over generic advice

Connect the user's question, enduring nature, current season, and present moment. Avoid interchangeable inspirational prose.

### 6.5 One meaningful move

Insight should resolve into a small, proportionate action, experiment, boundary, conversation, or reflection.

### 6.6 Calm over compulsion

The interface should create stillness and deliberate use. Avoid urgency loops, fear, streak pressure, and addictive divination behavior.

### 6.7 Depth through progressive disclosure

Offer a clear first insight, then allow users to enter deeper layers. Do not overwhelm first-time users with technical charts or metaphysical terminology.

### 6.8 Tradition with integrity

Preserve the internal logic of source systems while translating them carefully. Clearly distinguish calculated facts, interpretive synthesis, and reflective suggestions.

## 7. Experience Model

TUSO is centered on a single compass-like home experience.

### Guest journey

1. The user arrives with a question.
2. A breathing pause settles attention.
3. The user casts the I Ching through six throws.
4. TUSO first reveals the raw symbolic result.
5. The user may continue to personal meaning by providing birth details.
6. TUSO connects the question, cast, personal nature, and current season.
7. The user can read their Blueprint, read Personal Meaning, or create an account.

### Returning-user journey

The home experience combines:

- **Awareness:** what is present now;
- **Intention:** what the user is carrying or asking;
- **Orientation:** Nature, Season, and Move;
- **Oracle:** a deliberate press-and-hold ritual for meaningful questions.

## 8. Core Product Surfaces

### Compass Home

The central navigation and stateful ritual space. Four directional poles organize the product; the center invokes the Oracle.

### North / Personal Blueprint

An eight-part interpretation of the user's enduring nature:

1. See — Core nature
2. Perceive — Lens and interpretation
3. Speak — Voice and expression
4. Move — Natural rhythm of action
5. Build — Work, value, and resources
6. Connect — Relationship patterns
7. Balance — Tensions and blind spots
8. Return — Reflection to carry forward

Supporting layers may include Current Chapter, Blind Spots, and Life Areas.

### Season

A time-based view of the user's current decade, year, and nearer context. It describes conditions and timing without predicting events.

### Oracle / Personal Meaning

A six-throw I Ching ritual that begins with the user's question. It shows the primary and changed hexagrams, then offers a personal interpretation grounded in the user's Nature and Season.

### Move With Life

A concise suggestion for practice or action. Each Move should be timely, doable, non-coercive, and tied to the interpretation that produced it.

## 9. Interpretation Architecture

TUSO combines three distinct layers:

| Layer | Primary role |
| --- | --- |
| Bát Tự | Energetic structure, elemental dynamics, strength, timing, and change |
| Tử Vi | Life-area organization, roles, relationships, and contextual structure |
| I Ching | The present question, changing situation, tension, and movement |

The product should synthesize these layers without flattening them into one score. Technical terms can remain available as an optional source layer, while the default experience uses clear human language.

## 10. Voice and Response Standard

Every response should be:

- calm, direct, and specific;
- psychologically legible;
- respectful of uncertainty;
- free from fear-based claims;
- non-deterministic;
- proportionate to the user's question;
- explicit about what is observed versus inferred;
- concluded with a reflection or grounded next move.

Avoid:

- guaranteed outcomes;
- claims of destiny, doom, or supernatural certainty;
- medical, legal, or financial directives;
- dependency-forming language;
- vague praise that could apply to anyone;
- technical terminology without explanation.

## 11. MVP Definition

The MVP must prove one core loop:

> A person arrives with a real question, pauses, receives a symbolic result, understands why the question may be present now, and leaves with a personally meaningful orientation and next move.

MVP capabilities:

- question entry and breathing state;
- six-throw I Ching casting;
- raw primary and changed hexagram result;
- personal information capture;
- calculated personal profile and current timing context;
- Personal Blueprint;
- Personal Meaning for the question;
- one aligned Move;
- account creation and saved reading;
- responsive mobile-first experience;
- basic safety, privacy, and feedback mechanisms.

## 12. What TUSO Is Not

TUSO is not:

- a fortune-telling service;
- a horoscope content feed;
- a replacement for therapy, medicine, legal counsel, or financial advice;
- a deterministic personality test;
- a gamified daily prediction app;
- a social network in the MVP;
- a marketplace for readers or practitioners in the MVP.

## 13. Business Direction

The initial business model may combine:

- a free first orientation or launch-period gateway;
- paid Personal Meaning or full orientation package;
- premium North and Season reports;
- a seven-day Move With Life journey;
- eventual membership for ongoing seasonal guidance.

Monetization must never depend on fear, bad-luck warnings, artificial urgency, or withholding essential safety context.

## 14. Success Definition

TUSO succeeds when users report:

- “This named what I am actually experiencing.”
- “I understand myself and this moment more clearly.”
- “I know what I can do next.”
- “I feel more able to choose—not more dependent on the reading.”

Initial product success should be measured through completion, recognition, usefulness, and return for meaningful moments—not raw session frequency.

## 15. Current Boundaries and Open Questions

### Boundaries

- Web-first, mobile-responsive MVP.
- Vietnamese-first experience, with English-ready content architecture.
- Personal data and readings are private by default.
- Interpretations remain advisory and reflective.

### Open questions

- Final pricing and free-to-paid boundary.
- Whether account creation occurs before or after the first personal result.
- Exact scope of Tử Vi in the first calculation engine.
- Editorial review and quality-assurance workflow for generated interpretations.
- Retention model that supports reflection without encouraging compulsive checking.
- Appropriate crisis, high-stakes, and sensitive-topic response policies.
